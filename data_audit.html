A Context & why now

Ask the business owner / champion.

  1. What is broken or painful about data today? (one concrete story)
  2. Why fix this now (board, fundraising, burn, new hire delay, ops fire)?
  3. Who is the single owner of success on their side? Name, role, email.
  4. Who else must agree on KPI definitions (Finance, Ops, CS, Founder)?
  5. In two weeks, what would make them say “this was worth it”?

B Decisions & KPIs

Definition of truth for v1.

  1. What are the 3–5 questions leadership asks every week that currently take too long?
  2. Must-have KPIs for v1 (e.g. MRR, NRR, churn, CAC, activation, pipeline, refund rate, COGS, margin).
  3. For each KPI: how do they define it today? (even if messy)
  4. Where do teams disagree on a number today? Give one example.
  5. Which definitions must be locked for go-live vs can wait for phase 2?
PRD feed: success_metrics, kpi_definitions, deferred_metrics

D Outputs

  1. Who needs dashboards (roles + approx. seats)?
  2. Preferred BI: Metabase / Hex / Looker / Power BI / other / no preference?
  3. Name the first 1–3 dashboards for v1.
  4. Any alerts at launch (spend threshold, silent accounts, refund spike)?
  5. Natural-language “ask Vero” / Hex AI: need / nice / skip for v1?
PRD feed: dashboards, bi_tool, alerts, nl_layer

E Cloud, security, ownership

  1. Cloud preference: GCP / AWS / Azure / none? Existing account?
  2. Confirm: warehouse + infra in their cloud account (they pay the cloud bill)?
  3. SSO, VPC, DPA, or security questionnaire expected before access?
  4. Data residency (US / EU / other)?
  5. Any data that cannot leave a vendor or region?
PRD feed: cloud, ownership_model, security_requirements, residency

F Team & access

  1. Do they have a data engineer / analyst today? Hiring?
  2. Who grants credentials and IAM (name + Slack/email)?
  3. Typical blocker for vendor access (legal, IT, delay)?
  4. Can we get read/API access within 48 hours of kickoff? If not, what is realistic?
PRD feed: client_counterparts, access_risk, kickoff_unblockers

G Timeline & commercial

  1. Target go-live date for first dashboards?
  2. Hard dependencies before kickoff (security review, contract, cloud project)?
  3. Must-have in v1 vs explicitly phase 2?
  4. Budget: fixed quote from scope, or a band they can share?
  5. Retainer after build: stay on / handoff / undecided?

H. Close

  1. Confirm what you heard: top pain, v1 KPIs, approx. source count, go-live target.
  2. List open questions still unanswered.
  3. Agree who fills gaps by when.
  4. Next: Answer log locked → PRD draft → quote.

PRD Handoff checklist

When Answer log = Answers locked, the PRD should include:

  • Problem statement (from A)
  • Success metrics & locked KPI definitions (from B)
  • In-scope sources + cadences + backfill (from C)
  • Out-of-scope / phase 2 (from B + G)
  • Deliverables: warehouse ownership, pipelines, models, dashboards, alerts (from D + E)
  • Non-functionals: cloud, residency, security (from E)
  • Client counterparts & access plan (from F)
  • Timeline & milestones (from G)
  • Open risks & assumptions
  • Pricing inputs: source count by tier, dashboard count, BI/ingest choices → pricing calculator
