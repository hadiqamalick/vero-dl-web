## 12. Voice & messaging guardrails

**Commercial profile, not a student resume.**
- Lead with outcomes and ownership, not “passionate / curious / dedicated”.
- Name the problem: scattered tools, conflicting MRR, CSV culture.
- Name the product: Vero + Datum Labs.
- Prefer concrete metrics language: MRR, CAC, ROAS, activation, retention, LTV:CAC.
- First person, short paragraphs, bullets for “what I do”.

**Stay in analytics lane.**
- Don’t claim pipeline orchestration or Kubernetes ownership.
- Do claim metric ownership, dashboard UX for operators, stakeholder translation, QA when numbers conflict.

**Client naming.**
- Public case studies only when already on datumlabs.io or approved.
- Otherwise use outcome titles (“Lead-to-Revenue Dashboard for an AI Tax Platform”).

**Writing style (repo rule).**
- No em dashes. Prefer comma, colon, period, or parentheses.
