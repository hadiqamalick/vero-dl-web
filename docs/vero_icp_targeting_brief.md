## Who we target

Fast-growing **Seed / Series A SaaS** (and SaaS-like ops businesses) whose numbers live across CRM, billing, product, and ads, with **no platform / data team** to unify them. They are tool-rich and foundation-poor: every team has a different MRR, board prep is reconciliation, and AI or better dashboards are blocked on dirty data. When the stack is missing they lose time and trust, not a real-time trade. It's not one vertical. Target any business that fits the profile.

Proven across AI voice / usage-billed SaaS, tax tech, government-services SaaS, solar sales ops, and open to adjacent data-scattered SaaS (fintech, adtech, vertical SaaS, service businesses with multi-system ops).

## Firmographics

- **Industries:** B2B / PLG SaaS; AI / usage-billed products; tax / fintech / vertical SaaS; service SaaS with CRM + billing + support; performance-marketing-heavy growth teams. Adjacent: sales ops or lead-driven businesses with the same scattered-tool pattern.
- **Size:** ~10–150 employees. Below ~8 = often pre-product / no real volume yet. Above ~300–500 with a dedicated data/platform team = usually builds in-house (disqualify). Sweet spot matches Seed / Series A.
- **Geography:** US, UK, EU (NL proven), DACH, UAE open if English-speaking buyer and cloud access work. All markets where GCP / AWS / Azure customers are normal.
- **Stage:** Post-product, real revenue (or serious trial-to-paid motion), recently funded or under board/investor metric pressure. Growing source count (CRM + billing + product or ads at minimum).

---

## Strongest qualifier - their tech (technographics)

- Running **CRM + billing + at least one of** product analytics or ad platforms (e.g. HubSpot / Salesforce + Stripe / Chargebee / RevenueCat + PostHog / Mixpanel / Postgres + Google / Meta / LinkedIn Ads).
- Spreadsheet or Notion "warehouse"; manual exports; no dbt / Dagster / owned warehouse yet, or a half-finished stack nobody owns.
- Cloud account they control: **GCP, AWS, or Azure** (Vero deploys in their cloud; client pays the cloud bill).
- Want trusted metrics (MRR, CAC, retention, pipeline, unit economics) and live dashboards, often with a path to NL / AI answers on clean definitions.
- Optional but strong: already evaluating BigQuery / Snowflake / ClickHouse, dbt, Fivetran / dlt / Airbyte, Metabase / Hex / Looker.

Prioritise fit to the **scattered SaaS tools → one foundation** problem over pure real-time / Kafka needs (that profile is closer to Pulse).

---

## Prioritise these signals (timing)

1. **Hiring a Data / Analytics / Analytics Engineer** (or "first data hire") - #1 signal. They feel the pain now and are comparing hire vs outsource.
2. **Recent funding** (Seed / Series A) or board asking for metrics / dashboard this quarter.
3. Public mention of broken reporting, "source of truth," spreadsheet hell, or AI blocked on data quality.
4. Rapid add of tools (new CRM, billing, ads, support) without anyone owning the join.
5. Upwork / inbound asks for warehouse, dbt, pipelines, or "fractional data team."

## Who to contact (it's a group decision)

- **Economic buyer:** CEO / Founder / COO (especially founder-led &lt;50).
- **Champion / mobiliser:** Head of Ops / RevOps, Head of Growth / Marketing, CTO / tech co-founder (feels the pain, drives internal buy-in).
- **Technical evaluator:** CTO, senior engineer, or whoever will review dbt / Dagster / cloud IAM.

**Rule of thumb:** founder-led under ~50 employees; Ops / RevOps + CTO at 50–150. Reach the champion and the economic buyer in parallel.

### Persona hooks (use in outreach)

| Persona | Hook |
|---------|------|
| Founder / CEO | Just raised. Board wants metrics. Cannot wait six months for a hire to fix the spreadsheet. |
| Head of Ops / RevOps | Answers exist across tools. Getting them means filing a request and waiting on the wrong export. |
| CTO / Tech co-founder | Knows dbt and Dagster. Engineers are on product. Wants it built right, in their cloud, handed over cleanly. |
| Head of Growth / Marketing | Ads across channels. CAC, ROAS, and pipeline live in separate platforms. Every budget decision is a guess. |

Shared frame for all: **Vero is not a hire.** It is a specialist team with a repeatable playbook: warehouse, pipelines, models, dashboards live in days/weeks, owned by the customer.

## Don't waste time on (disqualifiers)

- **~300–500+ employees with a dedicated data / platform / SRE team** who prefer to build in-house.
- **Pre-product / no real tools yet** (no CRM + billing volume to unify).
- **No cloud account they will give access to**, or unwilling to own infra in their cloud.
- **Pure real-time / ClickHouse+Kafka platform rebuild** as the primary need with no SaaS reporting pain (route to **Pulse** instead).
- **No clear budget owner / chronic indecision** - disqualify, don't nurture forever.
- **Looking only for a BI dashboard on top of messy CSVs** with no appetite for warehouse + models + orchestration.
