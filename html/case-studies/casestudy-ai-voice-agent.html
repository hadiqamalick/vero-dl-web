The challenge

Twelve vendor bills. No idea what a minute cost — or which accounts were at risk.

AI Voice Agent SaaS sells call minutes, billed against AI and cloud infrastructure from a dozen separate vendors. Meanwhile, 85 customer accounts had no unified health view — CSMs were flying blind on utilization, engagement, and sentiment.

01
Twelve vendor bills, zero unit economics

GCP, Twilio, Azure, OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Deepgram — each billed in its own portal. Computing the true cost of a single call across Cloud Infra, LLM/AI, Speech, and Telephony categories meant manual CSV work. Cost per minute by customer segment was unknown.

02
Accounts going silent with no early warning

CSMs managed 85 accounts with no view of which ones were actively using their committed call minutes. Silent accounts — customers with zero product activity for 14 days or more — only surfaced when it was too late to intervene.

03
Frustration hidden inside support threads

Customer sentiment lived in Pylon Slack conversations — unread, unscored, and invisible in any dashboard. By the time a CSM knew an account was unhappy, the account was already a churn risk. No one had a way to see the frustrated accounts before they escalated.

Data sources connected

20+ systems. One warehouse.

Every system AI Voice Agent SaaS runs on — twelve AI and cloud vendor bills, per-region application databases, product engagement events, support conversations, and CRM — connected, normalized, and loaded on schedule into BigQuery.

What we built

COGS visibility and a CSM health view. Both automated.

Two parallel problems — cost transparency and customer health — solved with the same pipeline. Running on their own infrastructure, fully owned by their team.

01
Tokenized at the edge, centralized safely

A dedicated Dagster deployment runs inside each region — US and EU — extracting from that region's MongoDB, chat, and agent databases and tokenizing sensitive fields before anything leaves. Only tokenized data moves to the central legacy cluster, so data-residency rules hold while the company still gets one unified view.

Dagster · US & EU in-region tokenization data residency
02
COGS model — 12 vendors into one number

All twelve vendor bills and cloud costs loaded via dlt with schema evolution and row-count checks on every run. One dbt project — staging → intermediate → mart — joins them into true cost per minute by vendor, by cost category (Cloud Infra, LLM/AI, Speech, Telephony), by environment, and by customer segment. Pricing decisions now start from a real number.

dlt dbt COGS by segment
03
CSM health dashboard — per-account, automated

PostHog engagement (active days), Pylon support tickets, AI-derived sentiment from Pylon Slack conversations, HubSpot renewal dates, and Stripe payment status all joined into a single per-account health card. Silent accounts (14d+ no activity) and frustrated accounts (AI tone analysis: Frustrated/Angry) surface automatically — no manual checking required. Sensor-driven orchestration pushes updates back into Pylon and Linear via reverse ETL.

Dagster sensors Hex AI sentiment reverse ETL
How it unfolded

The cost was invisible. The at-risk accounts were invisible. Both are visible now.

Chapter 01 · Signal
12 vendors
Each billed separately. True cost of a call was unknown.

GCP, Twilio, Azure, OpenAI, ElevenLabs, Deepgram — each with its own portal and invoice. Computing the real cost of a voice minute across Cloud Infra, LLM/AI, Speech, and Telephony categories meant stitching CSVs by hand. The team was pricing and negotiating without knowing their unit economics.

Chapter 02 · Root Cause
No health view
85 accounts. No signal on who was at risk.

PostHog held engagement data. Pylon held support conversations. HubSpot held renewal dates. Stripe held payment status. None of it was connected. A customer could go silent for 14 days, rack up frustrated support threads, and miss their committed call minutes — and no CSM would know until the renewal conversation.

Chapter 03 · Outcome
Both visible
Cost per minute known. At-risk accounts surfaced automatically.

Twelve vendor bills unified into real COGS by category and customer segment. Per-account CSM health cards show utilization vs. committed minutes, PostHog active days, AI-derived sentiment from Pylon, and renewal status — all in one place. Silent and frustrated accounts appear without any manual checking.
