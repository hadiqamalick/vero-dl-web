The challenge

Five systems. Zero shared truth.

Voltera's data lived in the systems that ran the business — sales, operations, marketing, finance, and recruiting — but none of it was connected, and none of it could be trusted across teams.

01
Operational databases, used for reporting

Sales ran straight off the Postgres CRM and operations off the MSSQL ERP. Every analytical query competed with the live application and every report meant another fragile export.

02
Spend and sales never met

Facebook Ads spend lived in one platform, revenue in the CRM, and costs in Moneybird. Connecting marketing investment to actual customers was a manual, error-prone spreadsheet exercise.

03
Schemas that drifted silently

New columns appeared in source systems and broke downstream reports without warning. There was no contract, no monitoring, and no alert when a number went stale.

Every system Voltera runs the business on — sales, operations, marketing, finance, and recruiting — connected, normalized, and loaded on a schedule into BigQuery.

What we built

A complete, automated analytics platform.

The same repeatable Vero playbook, adapted to Voltera's sources and KPIs — running on their own infrastructure, fully owned by their team.

01
Connected all 5 sources into a central warehouse

Every system Voltera relies on now feeds automatically into BigQuery. dlt handles schema evolution, so new columns in the CRM or ERP are picked up without breaking downstream reports. The Postgres CRM refreshes every 15 minutes — close to real time for the sales team.

dlt Dagster on GKE BigQuery
02
Built a single, agreed-on set of KPIs

All business logic lives in one dbt project, layered staging → intermediate → mart. CRM revenue, ERP fulfilment, ad ROAS, and finance figures all share one definition the whole company trusts — no more reconciling exports before a meeting.

dbt
03
Wired ingestion to transformation, automatically

Sensor-driven orchestration means a successful CRM load triggers the CRM dbt models, and an ERP load triggers the ERP models — independently, so a heavy ERP refresh never drags the whole DAG along. Every failure posts straight to Slack.

Dagster sensors Slack alerts
