The challenge

Ads, deals, installations — all separate. No view of what a deal actually earned.

Voltera's business runs on a full chain: Meta ad → lead → quote → close → install. Each step lived in a different system. The team could see leads or quotes, but never the whole picture — and never the true margin.

01
Ad spend never connected to closed deals

Meta Ads showed leads. The CRM showed closes. But there was no way to trace which campaigns produced deals that actually made it to installation — let alone which channels had the lowest cost per closed deal.

02
Quote value isn't margin

Every signed deal had a gross quote value. But the real cost — planner commission, closer commission, installation, procurement, back office before and after install — was spread across the CRM, ERP, and finance system. True margin per deal was unknown.

03
Top and bottom performers invisible

Sales results lived in the CRM but cost and commission data was in other systems. There was no cross-system view of which closers were driving revenue, which agents had high cancellation rates, or where management should focus.

Data sources connected

5 systems. One warehouse.

Every system Voltera runs the deal journey on — CRM, ERP, Meta Ads, finance, and recruiting — connected, normalized, and loaded on schedule into BigQuery.

Domain Source What we pull
Sales / CRM Postgres CRM Leads, quotes, closes, agent assignments, planner and closer commission — incremental on updated_at, 15-minute cadence
Operations / ERP MSSQL ERP Installation records, procurement (inkoop), work preparation, back office costs before and after installation
Marketing Meta Ads (Facebook) Campaigns, ad spend, cost per lead, cost per click, generated leads — joined to CRM closes to compute true cost per closed deal
Finance Moneybird Invoices, payments, ledger entries — backoffice cost reconciliation per deal

How it unfolded

The funnel existed. The margin didn't — until the data connected it.

Chapter 01 · Signal
Ads → leads
Meta spend was generating leads. Nobody knew which became profitable deals.

Meta Ads was running, leads were coming in, and the sales team was closing. But the path from ad click to signed installation was invisible. Nobody could trace which campaigns generated deals that actually closed — let alone which ones made money after installation and back office costs.

Chapter 02 · Root Cause
No true margin
Quote value was known. Real cost per deal was in five different systems.

The CRM held quotes and closes. The ERP held installation and procurement costs. Moneybird held back office costs. Commission splits (planner vs. closer) were separate. Getting the real margin on any deal meant pulling data from every system by hand — so it almost never happened.

Chapter 03 · Outcome
Full funnel
From Meta ad to closed installation, one model. True margin per deal, per agent.

One dbt model joins all five systems into a per-deal record: quote value, all cost components, commission split, and the Meta campaign that sourced the lead. Management can now see which closers are driving revenue, which agents have high cancellation rates, and which ad spend actually converts to installed deals.
