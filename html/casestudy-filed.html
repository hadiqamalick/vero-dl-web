The challenge

Twelve systems. Zero shared truth.

Filed's data lived across marketing platforms, a product backend, CRM, billing, and event tracking — with no single layer connecting them. Every decision required manual reconciliation across tools.

01
No full-funnel visibility

Ad spend was in Facebook, LinkedIn, and Google. Leads were in HubSpot. Product usage was in Postgres and PostHog. Billing was in Sequence. No way to trace a customer from first ad impression to active workspace to invoice.

02
Pipelines broke with every release

Backend Postgres schemas changed with each product deploy. There was no contract, no monitoring, and no alert when a column disappeared. Reports went silently stale and nobody knew until a meeting started with wrong numbers.

03
Marketing spend had no attribution

Adspend per MQL was calculated manually in spreadsheets. Connecting campaign data to actual conversions, trials, and paid workspaces was a multi-hour exercise done inconsistently across teams.

What we built

A complete, automated analytics platform.

The same repeatable Vero playbook, adapted to Filed's full-funnel stack — running on Azure, fully owned by their team.

01
Connected all 12 sources into a central warehouse

Marketing channels run on Fivetran for low-maintenance, managed ingestion. Product databases and HubSpot run on dlt pipelines scheduled via Dagster — with schema evolution built in, so new Postgres columns no longer break downstream reports. PostHog and Sequence connect directly. Everything lands in Snowflake on schedule.

dlt Fivetran Dagster on AKS Snowflake
02
Built a Lead-to-Cash model the whole company trusts

All business logic lives in one dbt project — staging, intermediate, and mart layers. The Lead-to-Cash model connects ad spend in Google/Facebook/LinkedIn all the way through HubSpot deal stages to active workspaces and billing in Sequence. One definition. No more reconciling spreadsheets before a board meeting.

dbt Cloud
03
Shipped 5 production dashboards across every team

Lead to Cash for marketing and sales. Existing Customers and Trial Users for the product and account management team. Trial Funnel from PostHog events. Billing via Hex. All refreshed automatically — HubSpot every 30 minutes, product every hour, marketing every hour.

Metabase Hex 5 dashboards
04
Deployed on Azure with Slack alerting end to end

All services run in Docker containers on Azure Kubernetes Service. Dagster orchestrates every pipeline with automatic retries and failure notifications. Custom alerts fire to Slack when adspend per MQL exceeds $100 — and whenever any job fails.

Azure AKS Docker Slack alerts
🔔
Proactive alerting built in Dagster sends pipeline failure alerts to Slack immediately. A custom adspend-per-MQL alert fires hourly whenever the metric exceeds $100 — so the marketing team knows before the week ends, not after.
