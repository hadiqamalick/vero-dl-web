The challenge

The data was there. The trust wasn't.

GovPlus had data across customer support, payments, and reviews. But every team ran off their own export, every number needed manual verification, and nobody could agree on a single metric.

01
9 systems, no warehouse

Support in Intercom and CloudTalk. Payments in Chargebee and Paycom. Reviews on Google, Trustpilot, and BBB. No single place to bring it all together.

02
KPIs meant different things to different teams

Operations, Sales, and Finance each tracked their own version of the same metrics. Leadership syncs started with 20 minutes of reconciliation before any real conversation could happen.

03
Reputation data was invisible

Review volume, complaint trends, and 1-star spikes couldn't be connected to sales or support data. The team had no way to see the full picture.
